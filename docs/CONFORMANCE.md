## Iterative estimators (win/tie/loss)

A fitter or optimiser without a unique numerical optimum is judged on the objective it attains at its own solution, against the objective the oracle attains at its own, both evaluated by one shared function: the Gaussian log-likelihood for the fitters, the portfolio objective for the optimisers. A `win` means quantcore attained the better objective: the larger one where the objective is maximised, the smaller one where it is minimised. In the first table a `win` or `loss` is a gap of at least `0.001` in the objective's own units and anything smaller is a `tie`; this reporting granularity is separate from the decision rule below.

| `arima_fit` | `ar1` | 0 | 8 | 0 |
| `arima_fit` | `ar2` | 0 | 9 | 0 |
| `arima_fit` | `arma11` | 0 | 9 | 0 |
| `arima_fit` | `ma1` | 0 | 13 | 0 |
| `arima_fit` | `ma2` | 0 | 3 | 0 |
